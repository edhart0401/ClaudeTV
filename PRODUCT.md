# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform
web

## Users
Ed and his household select streaming apps or the PS4 from a sofa using an LG TV remote or pointer.

## Product Purpose
An ordinary removable launcher that makes seven everyday destinations easy to select and open.

## Operating Context
LG 49UH610V, webOS 3.4.3 / Chrome 38, fixed 1920×1080 TV canvas. Development previews run locally on the Mac. Installation and opening the launcher on the TV require Ed to say he is ready; he is currently watching something.

## Capabilities and Constraints
Netflix, Prime Video (lovefilm), BBC iPlayer, YouTube, Disney+, Nuvio Legacy, and PS4 on HDMI 2. Settings and Exit occupy opposite top corners. The physical LG Home button remains available. No root or TV system modifications. Upstream TypeScript stays read-only; compatibility code is JavaScript/JSX and is compiled to ES5. Large remote-friendly controls, exact app IDs, error feedback and robust rapid-key handling matter more than decorative motion.

## Brand Commitments
Use Ed's supplied service-logo files without distorting them; YouTube has a white background. No greeting, personal masthead, promotional content, tile numbers, descriptions, or decorative arrows. Ed rejected the previous disconnected tile/wallpaper composition and the full revolving 3D ring. His confirmed redesign structure is one large selected app plus a compact strip showing all seven. The requested direction is modern, dynamic and clean.

## Evidence on Hand
Supplied logo assets under public/logos and the selected Hubble Ultra Deep Field background under public/backgrounds. Existing launcher and package tests. No proprietary content artwork or streaming catalog API is part of this launcher.

## Product Principles
- Put selecting an app ahead of browsing decoration.
- Keep all seven destinations visible and recognisable.
- Confirm focus immediately; motion must not delay input.
- Respect the TV's actual browser and hardware limits.
- Preview locally before any authorised TV deployment.
